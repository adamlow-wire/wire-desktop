**SEC-008 popup question requiring native reproduction:** `mainProcess.ts` passes the top-level account `contents.getURL()` as `sourceUrl` into `AccountWindowPolicy`; `selectAccountPopup` accepts an empty `referrer.url` for normal noreferrer account links and may allow SSO/PiP/deep-link decisions from the same top-level source. Electron 43's [`setWindowOpenHandler` details](https://www.electronjs.org/docs/latest/api/web-contents#contentssetwindowopenhandlerhandler) list destination, frame name and outbound referrer but no opener-frame identity. This is a **hypothesis**, not an established bypass or permission to remove ordinary noreferrer links; reproduce with a local cross-origin child-frame fixture and deny-path assertions before changing product behavior. The [Electron sandboxed-iframe advisory](https://github.com/electron/electron/security/advisories/GHSA-9f4c-93c8-jc8g) does not list Electron 43.4.0 as affected and is not being claimed as this candidate's vulnerability.
